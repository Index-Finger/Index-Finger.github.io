A Short Story about a Rock and a Tree and a Bridge.

In this forest, many things collide. Seeds plunge their way through the floor. They grow and push back up as if they forgot something on their way down. As they retread their steps, the young things will push away their peers, shoving for sun, air. If it is weed or some shrub, the seedling will push hard to find unfiltered sun, even for a day. In time these fast growing ones are shunted, always, by a seed from a tree. These pull themselves up slowly. Tree sprouts fight green stems with the beginnings of warrior’s bark. When grown, they mold to fit their inherited volume of sky. Seeds of trees do not fight once they have grown. Those actions were of a prior plant, something lower to the ground. Up in the canopy trees do not fight. They know their place well above the rest and when they die they know another will be on the way. Let the groundlings shove and strangle, what matters their year long struggle? That is a heartbeat.
